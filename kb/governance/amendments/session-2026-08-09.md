## Proposed amendments (constitution)

1. **Amend Article 7 — telemetry-to-kb boundary.** Article 7 states secrets never enter any `kb/`, but the newly adopted friction pipeline (`kb/self-improvement.md` stage 2) has no mechanism enforcing that for its own output: `log-friction.sh` appends raw `tool_input` / `tool_response` / `reason` fields (truncated, unredacted) to the ledger, and the end-session reviewer is instructed to read that ledger and write proposals into `kb/improvements/` — a committed, non-gitignored path. Nothing today requires the reviewer to summarize root causes rather than quote ledger detail verbatim; the guarantee currently rests entirely on the synthesizing agent's judgment in a single pass, not on an enforced control. Add to Article 7: "Any pipeline that moves raw tool telemetry toward a `kb/` destination must summarize, not verbatim-quote, ledger detail fields." *Grounding: NIST AI RMF (Secure and Resilient — risk controls at data-handling boundaries); OECD AI Principles (robustness and security by design, not aspiration alone).* Security engineer proposed; ML safety researcher and ethicist concurred (the latter on data-minimization grounds — even non-secret PII in `tool_input`, e.g. file paths or usernames, shouldn't be quoted into a committed file by default). Carried 4–1.
