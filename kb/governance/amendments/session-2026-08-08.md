# Convention Session — 2026-08-08

Focus: stress-test the seed constitution (as committed in `4cb49ec`) for gaps. First session; no prior amendments.

## Proposed amendments

1. **New Article — Evaluation before adoption.** Agent-produced changes are checked against acceptance criteria derived from the governing spec before being adopted. *Grounding: NIST AI RMF (Measure).* Unanimous.
2. **New Article — Least privilege and containment.** Agents operate with the minimum access a task requires; secrets never enter any `kb/`; irreversible or outward-facing actions require prior owner approval. *Grounding: NIST AI RMF (Secure and Resilient).* Chair ruled this extends, not duplicates, Article 3. Unanimous.
3. **Amend Article 2 (Transparency) → Traceability.** Every consequential change must be traceable spec → change → rationale, not merely recorded. *Grounding: OECD AI Principles (transparency and explainability).* Unanimous.
4. **Amend Article 5 — scope triggers.** Every article must state what triggers it, so rules do not silently apply to all work. *Grounding: none in accepted principles — practicality argument, stated plainly.* Carried 4–1 (ethicist abstaining as process-neutral).

## Dissents (preserved verbatim in effect)

- **Ethicist:** user-facing specs should require a one-line human-impact statement; rejected on proportionality grounds for a playground repo. The ethicist holds this should be revisited if any project becomes user-facing.
- **Policy expert:** the constitution should be reviewed on a fixed cadence, not only on demand; rejected as premature. Holds that on-demand review biases toward drift going unnoticed.

## End-session review (proposed improvements to the convention itself)

- **Gap:** the skill gives no rule for choosing a session focus when the owner provides none; propose a default (review newest gaps, then oldest unreviewed dissents).
- **Gap:** no persona covers data governance/privacy; propose either a sixth persona or an explicit extension of the ethicist's brief.
- **Improvement:** session files should record the constitution version (commit hash) they reviewed — done in this file; propose adding it to the skill's steps.
- **Improvement:** the amendments index should show each session's status inline so pending review is visible at a glance.

None of the above is applied automatically; ratification is recorded below.

## Ratification (2026-08-08)

All four amendments **adopted** by the owner and merged into [constitution.md](../constitution.md): amendment 3 became Article 2 (Traceability), amendment 4 extended Article 5 (scope triggers), amendments 1–2 became Articles 6 and 7. The two minority proposals stay **rejected** per the panel's ruling: human-impact statements — disproportionate for a playground, revisit if any project becomes user-facing; fixed review cadence — premature, superseded in practice by the automated end-session review. With both sessions decided, the constitution's status moves from draft to adopted.
